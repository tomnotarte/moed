The Moed Method

Four quiet movements, one daily rhythm.

Every Moed follows the same simple order — so the practice becomes familiar, and the focus stays on what you're reading and hearing, not on the app.

01

Scripture

Open to a passage and write it out, slowly, in your own hand.

02

Observation

Notice what stands out — a word, a tension, a promise you hadn't seen before.

03

Application

Ask what it asks of you today, in one concrete, doable way.

04

Prayer

Close by talking to God about what you've just read, in your own words.
